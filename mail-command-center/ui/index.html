    Where do I find this?
    1. Open this account's Google Calendar (the Calendar switch in the account's header).
    2. Click the ⚙ gear, then Settings.
    3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click the calendar.
    4. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.

    Repeat for any other calendars of this account, one address per line. If a work account doesn't show a secret address, its administrator has turned that off.

    For calendars that aren't one of your Gmail accounts, like your personal iCloud calendar. Any calendar with an iCal (.ics or webcal://) link works.

    How do I get the address from iCloud?

    iPhone or iPad: open Calendar → Calendars → tap ⓘ next to the calendar → turn on Public Calendar → Share Link… → Copy.

    Mac: in Calendar, Control-click the iCloud calendar → Share Calendar… → tick Public Calendar → copy the address.

    iCloud.com: open Calendar, click the share icon next to the calendar → turn on Public Calendar → Copy Link.

    A public calendar link lets anyone who has it see the calendar's events, so keep it private. Turning Public Calendar off later stops the link from working.

    Outlook.com: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → copy the ICS link.
